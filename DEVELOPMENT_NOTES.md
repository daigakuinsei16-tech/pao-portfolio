# Paola Limin portfolio

Buildless, self-contained static portfolio. Hosted assets are in `dist/`.

## Content

- `dist/index.html`: page structure, experience, contact details and CV links.
- `dist/app.js`: six creative projects, project details, tabs, filters and confirmed brand credits.
- `dist/style.css`: responsive layouts, embedded fonts and reduced-motion support.
- `dist/assets/`: the supplied original covers, CV and locally hosted fonts.

The original cover files are preserved. SVG viewBoxes isolate the ARIES book face and the front of The Art of Braveity for display. The anonymous memoir treatment is illustrative; no author identity, manuscript, actual title or cover is included.

The additional brand credits were approved by the user: Amphenol LTW, Alorica Philippines, Zonli Home, Virtual Office Angels, Paseo Outlets, Alu General Merchandise, and First Aviation Academy. ARIES and the anonymous memoir lead both the hero shelf and creative project list.

Project genres, publication status and descriptions come from the user's supplied brief. Experience, professional samples and the testimonial come from the attached May 2026 CV. No traffic, ranking or performance metrics have been added.
